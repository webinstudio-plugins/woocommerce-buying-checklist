# Webin Vendor Workspace 0.1.10: native blocks and separate item-email review pages

October 12, 2026 · Product update from Webin Studio

Webin Vendor Workspace 0.1.10 is available for self-hosted WooCommerce stores. This update adds native editor blocks for vendor workflows and gives two item-email review pages distinct menu routes.

## What changed

The native blocks let site editors place vendor workflow interfaces through the WordPress editor. The update also corrects two review menus that previously shared a route. Each menu now opens its intended item-email review page. These changes concern editor placement and review navigation; they do not confirm customer email delivery or a payout.

The plugin covers vendor applications, product-change review, commission and refund records, and reviewed payout workflows. Review the workflow and infrastructure requirements with the developer responsible for your store before purchasing.
